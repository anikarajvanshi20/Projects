How Does National Well-Being Shape Global Music Consumption?

A Cross-National Behavioral Data Science Study — 2024

Anika Rajvanshi | Psychology & Biology | Ashoka University

Launch Interactive Dashboard Download Full Report

Research Overview

This project examines how economic uncertainty and social support influence large-scale cultural behavior using global Spotify streaming patterns and World Happiness Report indicators across 20 countries.

Research Questions

  • Does economic uncertainty increase repetitive music listening?
  • Does low social support drive consumption of positive (upbeat) music?
  • Does national stress increase nostalgia-driven listening?

Methods

I constructed composite socioeconomic indices using z-score normalization and feature engineering across GDP, social support, corruption, and life evaluation metrics. Behavioral indicators were extracted from Spotify streaming data including repetition score, positive music share, and nostalgia index.

Key Findings

  • Economic uncertainty correlates positively with repetitive listening behavior
  • Higher uncertainty trends toward increased positive music consumption
  • Nostalgia preference decreases under economic stress, contrary to expectations
